AfriSchool
Overview

AfriSchool is a school management platform designed to bring together the different aspects of school life in a single application. The platform aims to centralize academic management, timetables, grades, school life and a dedicated social network for the school community.

Challenge

The main challenge was to bring together a large number of school-related features into a single application while keeping the experience coherent and easy to use. The project covers a broad scope, from academic management to social interactions between members of the school community.

Solution

We designed a unified school management platform capable of centralizing the different aspects of school life. The frontend was built around the different use cases of students, teachers and the school administration.

My Role

I worked primarily as a Frontend Developer and UX/UI Designer. Since the backend architecture and technical decisions were still being defined, I worked with fictional data to develop and validate the frontend experience and the different user interfaces.

Technologies

React, Tailwind CSS

Key Features
- School management
- Timetable management
- Grade management
- School life management
- School social network
Result / Current Status

The project is still under development. My frontend and UX/UI work is currently available to showcase through screenshots.

GECA
Overview

GECA, short for Gestion Électronique du Courrier Administratif, is a project focused on digitizing and streamlining the management of administrative correspondence. The concept was designed with public administration in mind, particularly the Senegalese administration.

Challenge

Administrative correspondence can still involve numerous manual steps, physical document transfers and waiting periods between different departments. This makes the process slower, harder to track and more difficult to organize efficiently.

Solution

The project aims to provide a centralized digital workflow for managing administrative correspondence. The idea is to make it easier to register, transfer, track and process official documents while reducing the dependency on manual processes.

My Role

My role was focused on UX/UI design. I worked on the concept and interface design, but I did not have the opportunity to develop the application.

Technologies

UX/UI Design / Figma

Key Features
- Administrative mail management
- Digital document workflow
- Centralized correspondence
- Document tracking
- Administrative process management
Result / Current Status

The project was never developed into a finished application. The UX/UI design is still in progress. The original idea was to develop the concept further and potentially present it to the Senegalese administration.

LAM INFORMATIQUE
Overview

LAM Informatique is an IT consulting and training company based in Dakar, Senegal, providing technology consulting, professional training and technical support in areas such as Cloud Computing, DevOps, cybersecurity, networking and software engineering.

The project consisted of designing and developing the company's institutional website to present its services, training programs, expertise and team, while providing visitors with a clear way to discover the company's offers and get in touch.

Challenge

The main challenge was to structure a large amount of information — IT services, certifications, training programs, consulting offers and company information — into a clear and professional digital experience.

The website also needed to communicate LAM Informatique's technical positioning while remaining accessible to visitors who may not have a strong technical background.

Solution

I designed and developed a responsive corporate website that centralizes LAM Informatique's services, training programs, methodology, team and contact information.

The website uses structured sections and reusable components to present the company's different offers while maintaining a consistent user experience across the platform.

My Role
